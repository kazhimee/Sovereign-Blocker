# Firefox Add-ons release notes — 5.1.0

Sovereign Blocker now gives you control over which services are filtered from search results. Choose individual Google, Apple, Microsoft, social media, and other platform services in the full-page settings screen. Nothing is selected by default, and your custom keyword list starts empty.

The toolbar popup now shows the filtering status and opens settings. Add optional keywords, remove them whenever you like, or pause all filtering with the master switch. Your preferences are saved locally in Firefox.

Supported search pages include Brave Search, Google Search, Bing Search, and SearX.
